What Users May Contact Support About

Feel free to reach out to our team regarding:

  • Crashes & Technical Problems: Game freezes, startup issues, black screens, or unexpected error messages.
  • Lost Progress: Inquiries regarding locally saved high scores, star criteria, or level unlock recovery.
  • Advertising or Rewarded Continue Issues: Problems where video continues fail to load, ad display glitches, or questions about ad frequency.
  • Privacy & Consent Questions: Questions about in-game telemetry settings, Google UMP choices, or data subject inquiries.
  • General Feedback & Suggestions: Ideas to improve block generation, puzzle balance, or user interface feel.

What to Include in Your Bug Report

To help us reproduce and resolve technical issues quickly, please include:

  • Game Version: e.g., 0.1.0 (viewable in Settings → About Game).
  • Device Model: e.g., Samsung Galaxy S23, Google Pixel 8, OnePlus 12.
  • Android OS Version: e.g., Android 14, Android 15, Android 16.
  • Description of Problem: What happened and what did you expect to happen?
  • Steps to Reproduce: What actions led to the issue? (e.g., "Placed a 3x3 block while clearing row 7 in Adventure Chapter 1 Level 10").
  • Screenshots / Video Clips: If available, visual attachments help identify UI or board layout glitches.
Security & Privacy Notice: Please never include passwords, financial data, or sensitive personal information in your emails. Our support team will never ask for payment details or credentials.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I turn sound effects or vibration on or off?

From the Home screen, tap the Settings (Gear) icon in the header. Use the dedicated switches for Sound effects and Vibration. Your preferences are saved immediately on your device.

How do I manage analytics or advertising choices?

Open Settings and scroll to Privacy & Data. Here you can tap Allow Analytics or Deny Analytics at any time. You can also tap Advertising Choices to adjust your Google ad consent preferences.

How do I reset my game progress or delete saved data?

Because Block Blast saves progress locally on your device without server-side cloud backups, you can reset all progress by clearing app storage:
Navigate to Android Settings → Apps → Block Blast → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage. The next time you open the game, it will start completely fresh.

Why didn't a rewarded continue video appear when my game ended?

Rewarded continue ads require an active internet connection and available video inventory from Google AdMob. If your device is offline or the ad network has no fill, the game over screen concludes normally without the continue offer.

Can I play Block Blast offline?

Yes. Block Blast is built as an offline-first single-player puzzle game. You can play Classic mode, Endless challenges, and all unlocked Adventure levels without internet access.
