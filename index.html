Core Game & Privacy Principles

  • No Personal Player Accounts: Block Blast does not require or feature user registration, logins, passwords, or social media linking.
  • Offline-First Puzzle Play: Core gameplay, level scores, unlocked chapters, stars, and high scores are saved locally on your device in private application storage.
  • Player-Controlled Telemetry: In-game analytics and diagnostic reporting are optional and consent-gated. You can grant, deny, or modify your telemetry choice anytime via in-game Settings.
  • Transparent Advertising: Rewarded continue opportunities are voluntary and opt-in. In-game advertising choices can be managed through Google's consent dialog.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade player data to data brokers or third parties.
In-Game Access Within Block Blast, players can access these documents directly at any time by opening Settings → Support & Info.
